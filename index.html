How this works
A scheduled scan runs automatically every weekday morning (7am NZT) on GitHub's servers — no action needed from you. It scores ~900 stocks from the S&P 500, S&P 400, and NASDAQ 100 using Yahoo Finance data, then publishes the results here. Open this tab on any device and the latest data loads automatically.
Data source: Yahoo Finance  ·  Scored by: Hatch Research scanner  ·  View scan history on GitHub
